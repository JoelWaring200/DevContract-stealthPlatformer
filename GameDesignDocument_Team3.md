# **Objective Statement**
The objective of this prototype is to explore how enemy patrol patterns and level layout influence a player's ability to navigate a 2D platforming environment without being caught.

The prototype aims to determine whether players can observe enemy movement, identify safe opportunities to progress, and use platforms and gaps to navigate the level successfully.

Success will be evaluated by whether players understand the objective, can recognize enemy patrol patterns, and can complete the level through careful movement and timing.
## **Design Goals**
- Create a playable 2D stealth platforming level.

- Encourage players to observe enemy movement before progressing.

- Use platforms and gaps to influence navigation and timing.

- Establish a clear objective and consequence for being detected.

- Evaluate whether the level provides a fair but challenging stealth experience.
## **Game Mechanic**
The primary mechanic being explored is stealth-based navigation. Players must move through the level while avoiding enemies and finding opportunities to progress without being detected.

**Enemy Behaviour**

Enemies patrol back and forth throughout the level. If an enemy spots the player, the level resets, requiring the player to attempt the level again.

**Player Failure**

Detection by an enemy triggers a level reset. This creates a clear consequence for failing to remain undetected and encourages players to observe enemy movement and time their progress carefully.

# **Limitations**
**Potential limitations include:**

Limited enemy behaviour: Enemies patrol back and forth, limiting the variety of stealth challenges.

Limited level scope: The prototype uses platforms, gaps, and enemies to explore navigation within a single level.

Limited stealth options: Additional mechanics, such as hiding or distracting enemies, are not confirmed as implemented but would be good for immersion.

Limited testing: Further playtesting required
